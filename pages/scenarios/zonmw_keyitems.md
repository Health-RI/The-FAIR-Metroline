## Scenario Overview
ZonMw requires good data management and implementation of the FAIR principles (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable and Reusable) throughout all funded projects. The aim is to ensure that research data can be reused to verify results, support new research and inform policy, while being usable by both people and computers. This policy is integrated into every stage of the grant process, from the application and grant conditions to project reporting.

This page provides guidance on the FAIR aspects of ZonMw's key items, helping you complete the key items table.

Before getting started:
* When needed, enlist the support of a FAIR data steward, if possible. Read more about this topic on [Metroline Step: Have a FAIR data steward on board]({{site.baseurl}}/metroline_steps/have_a_fair_data_steward_on_board).
* If there are gaps in your (team’s) knowledge, considering following training. Read more about this topic on [Metroline Step: Organise training]({{site.baseurl}}/metroline_steps/organise_training).
