**2016.** Publication of [The FAIR Guiding Principles for scientific data management and stewardship](https://www.nature.com/articles/sdata201618) by Wilkinson et al.

**2016–2023.** Development of FAIRification workflows such as [FAIR Plus](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-023-02167-2) and [De Novo](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s13023-021-02004-y), providing more concrete approaches for implementing the FAIR principles.

**Early 2023.** Comparison of FAIRification workflows to identify common FAIRification steps and support efficient large-scale implementation ([Dos Santos Vieira, et al.](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7867293)). This work resulted in the well-known FAIR Metroline visual.

**Mid 2023.** Following stakeholder requests to make FAIR accessible to a broad audience, Health-RI decides to develop the FAIR Metroline into practical guidance for researchers, data professionals, clinicians, trainers, policy makers and IT staff.

**2024.** Development of the FAIR Metroline guidance pages begins. Experts from the hub and nodes co-develop content. Initial development takes place in the Health-RI Confluence environment.

**Early 2025.** Following stakeholder requests for improved accessibility and usability, FAIR Metroline website development begins. The website is developed on GitHub using the ELIXIR Toolkit theme. In parallel, the complementary “Tool Assemblies” project is initiated, focusing on guidance on tools and their integration into workflows.

**End 2025.** Initial versions of most FAIR Metroline guidance pages are completed. The Tool Assemblies project is integrated with FAIR Metroline, enabling direct references to relevant tools and creating a more cohesive and practical user experience.

**2026.** Continued improvement of the FAIR Metroline website, with a focus on usability. Development of use cases to provide practical, scenario-based guidance. User panels are established to gather structured feedback on user experience and interface improvements.
