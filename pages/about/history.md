**2016.** Publication of [The FAIR Guiding Principles for scientific data management and stewardship](https://www.nature.com/articles/sdata201618) by Wilkinson et al.by Wilkinson et al.

**2016–2023.** Development of FAIRification workflows such as [FAIR Plus](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-023-02167-2) and [De Novo](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s13023-021-02004-y), providing more concrete approaches for implementing the FAIR principles.

**Early 2023.** Comparison of existing FAIRification workflows to identify common FAIRification steps and support efficient implementation at scale, published in [Critical steps towards large-scale implementation of the FAIR data principles](https://zenodo.org/records/7867293) by Dos Santos Vieira et al. This work resulted in the now well-known FAIR Metroline visual.

**Mid 2023.** In response to stakeholder requests to make FAIR accessible to a broad audience, Health-RI decides to translate the FAIR Metroline into practical guidance for researchers, data professionals, clinicians, trainers, policymakers and IT staff. This work becomes part of Health-RI’s roadmap for the phased implementation of FAIR data practices across the Dutch health and life sciences sector.

**2024.** Development of the [FAIR Metroline guidance pages]({{site.baseurl}}/fair_metroline_steps) begins. Experts from the [Health-RI hub and nodes](https://www.health-ri.nl/en/about-health-ri/our-experts/regional-nodes) co-develop content. Initial development takes place in the Health-RI co-working Confluence environment.Confluence environment.

**Early 2025.** Following stakeholder requests for improved accessibility and usability, [FAIR Metroline website]({{site.baseurl}}/index) development begins. The website is developed on GitHub using the [ELIXIR Toolkit theme](https://elixir-belgium.github.io/elixir-toolkit-theme/). In parallel, the complementary “Tool Assemblies” project is initiated, focusing on guidance on tools and their integration into workflows. Alignment with the [TDCC-LSH Interoperability project](https://tdcc.nl/lsh-interoperability/) and relevant international initiatives such as the [RDA MaLDReTH](https://www.rd-alliance.org/groups/mapping-the-landscape-of-digital-research-tools-ii-maldreth-ii/activity/) working group and the [ELIXIR Interoperability platform](https://elixir-europe.org/platforms/interoperability) is established.

**End 2025.** Initial versions of most FAIR Metroline guidance pages are completed. The Tool Assemblies project is integrated with FAIR Metroline, enabling direct references to relevant [tools]({{site.baseurl}}/toolassemblies/tool_selection) and creating a more cohesive and practical user experience.

**2026.** Continued improvement of the FAIR Metroline website, with a focus on usability. Development of use cases to provide practical, scenario-based guidance. User panels are established to gather structured feedback on user experience and interface improvements. Alignment and mapping with the ELIXIR FAIRification Framework has been done.
